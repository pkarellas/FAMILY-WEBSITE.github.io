From the photos and videos I've seen, the Coeur d'Alene / Post Falls area looks absolutely beautiful and offers a surprisingly large amount of activities to do for families.
To get a better sense of the area, I started a discussion on the Coeur d'Alene Reddit community asking locals for their favorite restaurants and family-friendly activities.
I also spoke with several people I know who have visited the area.
This webpage is a collection of all of that information in one convenient place.

Disclaimer: This page is not intended to suggest that this is exactly what we should do during the trip.
We may choose to do several of these activities, just a few, or none at all.
No pressure.
The purpose of this page is simply to serve as a central resource for ideas, information, and easy navigation while exploring the area.
For quick navigation, click the image in the upper-right corner of the page.

PLACES TO EAT

  • IZZY'S COMFORT KITCHEN

    Multiple Reddit responders suggested this restaurant.
    It's a comfort food restaurant meaning dishes such as fried chicken, burgers, shrimp and grits, and BBQ.
    Izzy's also has multiple gluten free options.
    This one is a little more highend but comes very highly recommended.

  • CAFE CARAMBOLA

    Many responded saying this was the place to be for lunch.
    This restaurant serves all Latin American and Spanish dishes.
    They can sell out, so it's best to go early.

  • MOONTIME

    This is a popular sports bar in the area.
    Moontime is a pub that focuses mostly on beer and wine.
    Would be fun on a slow night.

  • HUDSONS BURGER

    This is a must try hamburger place that has been around since 1907.
    Stools at the counter, no tables.
    They don’t have much on the menu beyond hamburgers and pies for dessert; no fries, no chips.

  • EMBER BY THE LAKE

    Ember is a little bit of a hike.
    It's located in Hauser Idaho so it's about 30 minutes away.
    Supposedly, they have decent pizza.
    Ember also has a great family-friendly outdoor area.

  • CEDARS

    This restaurant was not suggested but I included because it's a floating restaurant.
    This one is definitely pricy.
    We are able to dock the pontoon boat at the restaurant, definitely a selling point for me.

  • BIER HAUS

    This one is for Steve.
    Has German beer on tap.
    Serves the usual German food such as Schnitzel, Spaetzel, and German potato salad.

The Coeur d'Alene and Post Falls area has two amusement parks, both conveniently located just a short drive from downtown.

Silverwood Theme Park is significantly larger than Triple Play but admission is more expensive.
Silverwood is geared more toward older children, but it also features numerous rides and activities for younger kids.
In other words either place is fine for everyone.

  • SILVERWOOD AMUSEMENT PARK

    Silverwood theme park is located in Athol, Idaho which is about 31 minutes away from us.
    It has over 65 rides, slides, shows and attractions.
    There are four roller coasters, a water park, a steam engine train, live entertainment, and restaurants.

  • TRIPLE PLAY & RAPID REEF

    Triple Play is located in Hayden Lake, Idaho which is around 18 minutes away from us.
    This one is more of an arcade with indoor bowling, putt putt golf, zip-line, and also has a VR experience.
    Additionally, there is a waterpark with slides and a wave pool.

The Coeur d'Alene area offers countless opportunities for hiking.
With so many hiking trails to choose from, it would be a shame not to spend at least a little time enjoying what mother nature has to offer.

There are far too many trails to include on this page, so I've selected four that are suitable for everyone.
Each is approximately one mile in length this is roughly a 20 to 30 minute walk at a comfortable pace.
While some trails are a bit more challenging than others, they are all manageable.

FERN AND SHADOW FALLS
Popular hike to a series of waterfalls.
Considered a beginner hike approximately 0.8 miles.
Downside that it's located approximately 2 hours away.
I was told to go early in the morning because it gets crowded.
MIRABEAU POINT LOOP
This route goes around the Mirabeau Point Park area which is 1.1 miles.
It's a flat trail with small hills and takes you through a forest.
There is a view of Mirabeau Pond.
BEAUTY BAY LOOKOUT AND LOOP
This is a 0.5 mile loop around a very scenic trail.
It is supposed to take an average of 15 min to complete.
I was told best times to visit this trail are July through November, so perfect timing!
TUBBS HILL TRAIL
This path loops around Tubbs Hill just south of Coeur d'Alene.
This one is longer at 2.2 miles but it's a very easy walk.
We can walk from downtown, pass McEuen Park, and get right onto the trail.
There is a beach on the southwestern side of the loop.

There are a couple of wine tasting rooms conveniently located in downtown Coeur d'Alene if anyone is interested in sampling a few local wines.

That said, if we decide to visit either one, I can't guarantee the quality of the wine so don't hold me responsible!
They're tasting rooms, not wineries, but they could still be a fun stop while exploring downtown.

  • COEUR D'ALENE CELLARS


    I spoke with an employee and they gave me the run down on the place.
    They get their grapes from Washington and process them in town.
    He also said there is live music on Saturday of the week we are in town.

  • RIVAURA ESTATE VINEYARDS & WINERY

    Rivaura is also located in downtown CDA.
    They have a large selection of wine.
    I'm told their Syrah is very popular.
    As far as I can tell they don't have live music during the time of our stay.

ADDITIONAL THINGS TO DO!

McEuen Park

Looked like a nice park to take the kids.
Has a playground area.
It's located in downtown CDA walking distance to restaurants and bars.

Big Red Barn

Cute indoor petting zoo.
There is a sloth encounter.
The Big Red Barn has alot of different animals including bunnies, goats, and ferrits.

CDA Resort Golfing

This is definitely an activity just for Doug, Dianne, and possibly Steve.
I included it because it looks like a very pretty course.
I'm not sure if you have to stay at the resort to play though.

CDA Tour

This is a night tour with Twilight Rydables Guided Tours.
We ride on a sequined unicorn while doing the tour.
The sights are narrated through a wireless audio system.

Carousel

The carousel is right next to the main beach and city park.
Another piece of history in Coeur D'Alene.
The original was built in the 1940's and was located on a pier over the lake.

Children's Museum

This is probably be more for me and Jen.
Mobius Discovery Center is a well reviewed kids museum located in Spokane.
